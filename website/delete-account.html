First Milk Venture (FMV)

Request account or personal data deletion

This page is for the First Milk Venture customer app and does not require signing in.

Delete your account

  1. Email admin@firstmilkventure.com from your registered email address, with the subject FMV Account Deletion.
  2. Include your registered account email or phone number and confirm that you want your account and associated personal data deleted.
  3. FMV will verify ownership and communicate any outstanding orders, refunds or required retention before processing the request.
Request account deletion by email

Delete selected personal data without closing your account

Email the same address with the subject FMV Personal Data Deletion and describe the information you want removed, such as an unused address or optional profile information. We will verify ownership and explain any information needed to continue your requested services.

Request personal data deletion

Data deletion and retention

Account credentials, profile information, saved addresses and optional personal information are subject to deletion when no longer required. Order, invoice, payment, refund, dispute and security records may be retained where necessary for legal obligations, fraud prevention or unresolved transactions. Backup copies may remain until scheduled expiry. FMV will explain applicable retention periods and exceptions when responding to your request; a fixed backup expiry period has not yet been published.

Never send passwords, OTPs, card security codes or UPI PINs. Uninstalling the app does not delete your account.

Contact: admin@firstmilkventure.com
