FIRST MILK VENTURE

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026 · Version 1.0

Freshness You Can Trust

1. Scope

This notice applies to First Milk Venture (FMV) customer applications, customer portal and related ordering, subscription, delivery and support services. FMV is the business responsible for the processing described here.

2. Applicable law

We respect applicable Indian privacy, information technology, consumer protection and e-commerce laws, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and its rules as applicable and in force. Mandatory statutory rights are not waived.

3. Information collected

Depending on the service you use, we may process name, email, phone, account identifiers, authentication records, delivery address and instructions, orders, subscriptions, invoices, payment references, returns, support messages, IP address, device and session information and security logs. Optional media or location information is collected only if the relevant feature is used and permission is provided.

4. Sources

Information comes from your registration, purchases, communications, authorised device permissions, service interactions and relevant payment or delivery providers.

5. Purposes

We use information to administer accounts, authenticate users, fulfil orders, manage subscriptions and deliveries, reconcile payments, handle refunds and complaints, prevent fraud, maintain service reliability and meet legal obligations.

6. Consent and choices

Where consent is required we seek specific informed consent. You may withdraw optional consent by contacting us. Withdrawal does not invalidate prior lawful processing. Required information may be necessary to provide requested services. This notice is not blanket consent to future processing.

7. Authentication

Keep credentials confidential. Never share passwords or verification codes with unauthorised persons. Report suspicious activity to us. We may restrict suspicious sessions proportionately.

8. Payments

Where supported, payment providers may process payment details independently. FMV uses transaction references, amounts and status for reconciliation and refunds. Do not send banking passwords, UPI PINs, card security codes or banking OTPs to FMV.

9. Wallet and store credit

If introduced or enabled, wallet and store-credit information may include balances, credits, debits and redemption records for reconciliation and fraud prevention. Applicable terms and legal requirements will be disclosed before use.

10. Rewards

Future loyalty programmes may process eligible purchase history, points, redemption and referral information. Programme terms and any additional privacy disclosures will be provided before activation.

11. Delivery and location

Delivery staff may receive necessary customer contact details, address, instructions and order details for assigned deliveries. These must not be used for unrelated personal contact or marketing. Any location feature must be limited to its disclosed purpose.

12. Permissions

Where available, location assists delivery address selection, camera and photos support user-selected attachments, microphone supports user-initiated voice messages, and notifications communicate service updates. Manage permissions in device settings. Unanticipated sensitive access requires additional prominent disclosure and consent where applicable.

13. Support and AI

Support communications may be used to resolve enquiries. Do not submit banking credentials or unnecessary confidential information. If external AI processing is introduced, material data transfers and purposes will be disclosed before use. Contact us for human assistance.

14. Advertising

FMV may display its own offers. Optional marketing may be declined by contacting us; necessary service notices may continue. Third-party advertising or tracking, if introduced, requires updated disclosures and appropriate privacy choices before activation.

15. Cookies and storage

Cookies or local storage may support sessions, preferences, carts and security. Non-essential tracking requires appropriate disclosure and consent where applicable.

16. Sharing

Information may be shared as necessary with hosting, database, authentication, email, payment, delivery and support providers, professional advisers and authorities where lawfully required. FMV does not sell personal information. Provider access should be limited to the relevant service.

17. International processing

Infrastructure providers may process data outside India. Applicable transfer restrictions and safeguards must be respected.

18. Security

FMV is committed to reasonable technical and organisational safeguards proportionate to risk, including access restrictions, secure communications, authentication, monitoring and recovery measures as appropriate. No system is absolutely secure; this does not limit statutory obligations.

19. Incidents

We will investigate suspected personal data breaches, take appropriate containment and corrective measures and notify affected individuals and authorities as required by applicable law.

20. Retention

Account data is retained as necessary for active services and deletion processing; order and payment records as required for accounting, tax and disputes; support and security records as necessary for resolution and protection. Verification codes are intended for short-term authentication. Backup copies may persist until scheduled expiry. Data no longer needed should be deleted or irreversibly anonymised. Specific retention periods will be updated once verified.

21. Account deletion

To request permanent account and associated data deletion, email admin@firstmilkventure.com with the subject FMV Account Deletion. Include your registered email or phone, not your password or OTP. We may verify your identity and explain necessary legal, accounting, security or dispute-related retention exceptions. Uninstalling the app does not delete your account. An in-app deletion facility is required for Google Play compliance and must be verified before release.

22. Privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, request access, correction, completion, erasure, consent withdrawal, grievance resolution or nomination by contacting us. Requests will be handled within applicable legal timelines.

23. Children

Customer purchasing services are intended for adults aged 18 or older. Where children's data is processed, applicable parental consent and child protection requirements must be followed. Contact us regarding improperly collected children's information.

24. Fraud prevention

Relevant account and transaction records may be used to detect payment manipulation, duplicate refunds, identity misuse or reward abuse. Protective measures should be proportionate and may be disputed through our contact channel.

25. Business changes

In a lawful restructuring or business transfer, information may be transferred only as permitted by law, with appropriate safeguards and notice where required.

26. External services

Independent external websites and payment services may have their own notices. FMV remains responsible for its own processing and applicable obligations concerning its processors.

27. Policy changes

We will publish material changes and updated effective dates. Additional consent will be sought where required; continued use is not a substitute for legally required affirmative consent.

28. Privacy and grievances

Contact First Milk Venture at admin@firstmilkventure.com for privacy requests, complaints and account deletion. You may approach the competent authority where applicable. Additional official business contact details will be published when available.

29. Governing law

Applicable Indian law governs this notice without restricting mandatory rights in other applicable jurisdictions. Nothing excludes non-excludable liability or remedies.

30. Commitment

FMV is committed to transparency, data minimisation, purpose limitation, appropriate safeguards and respect for privacy rights. Future features described here are not representations that they are currently operational.
